How I recruit

Quality comes from a clear target and a real screening step.

A profile only shows what someone chose to write. So I spend my effort where it counts: defining what good looks like, then checking every candidate against it.

1

Define quality

Agree with the client on must-haves, nice-to-haves and deal-breakers, ideally with one strong and one weak example profile.

2

Source widely

Boolean and X-ray searches, LinkedIn filters, referrals, community groups and a saved pool of past good candidates.

3

Screen

A short call or message to confirm skills, availability, salary range and motivation, plus a CV for the full background.

4

Present

A small shortlist with notes on fit and risks, so the client decides on facts and not on a link.

Sample Boolean search

An example for a sales role in property, to show the approach.

("sales manager" OR "sales supervisor" OR "sales team lead") AND ("real estate" OR property OR developer) AND (Batangas OR Cavite OR Laguna OR "Metro Manila") NOT intern

My screening questions

Five questions that turn a profile into a real candidate.

  1. What is your current role and main responsibilities?
  2. Which skills or tools do you use most day to day?
  3. What salary range are you looking for?
  4. When could you realistically start?
  5. What would make you consider a move right now?
